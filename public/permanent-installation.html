What are you adding?

Lights, switches, plugs and radiator valves are usually Zigbee. Choose one, then follow the three steps.

  1. Press the search button above. The hub starts listening for new devices.
  2. Put the device in pairing mode. Usually hold its button for about 5 seconds until its light blinks.
  3. Wait a few seconds. The device appears here by itself. You do not need to refresh.

Found nearby

Each device appears once. Choose the one you just put in pairing mode.
